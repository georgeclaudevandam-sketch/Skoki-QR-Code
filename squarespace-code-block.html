Printing tips
  • Send the SVG to your printer if they accept it. It stays sharp at any size.
  • Keep the white border around the code. Phones need it to find the edges.
  • Print dark on a light background. Light codes on dark cards often fail to scan.
  • Test the printed proof with both an iPhone and an Android phone before ordering.
